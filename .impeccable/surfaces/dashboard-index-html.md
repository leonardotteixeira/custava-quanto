Scope: dashboard/index.html, the whole public page. Mode: Read (the visitor understands something), with exploration.

Audience/job: Brazilian readers (first-timers, one-hand mobile, verifiers) who want to see how prices and indicators changed since 2019, with context, without a partisan verdict. Constraints: real data only, no browser-side economics, political colors as identifiers only, Fraunces/Inter + Brand Book palette + symbol + name preserved.

Structure: brief-pinned editorial sequence (masthead, cover, index, lead story, evidence spread, context, time capsule, governments, archive, notes). Surface roll f300cfd1 dealt my candidates 7/5/6 (diptych lead, ledger-index, capsule-edition); the brief's pinned sequence and asymmetry requirement beat the diptych lead; the ledger-index (5) became the product navigation and the capsule-edition (6) the time-capsule spread.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A data publication, not a panel: content sets the composition; the page refuses the chip-rail + KPI-card + boxed-chart dashboard arrangement.
OWN-WORLD: petrol ink on archive paper, one amber for "the number that matters", double nameplate rules, hairline column rules, Fraunces display at newspaper scale, Inter numerals, no cards, no pills.
STORY: reader grasps the idea (prices then vs now), picks an item from a typographic table of contents, reads the lead number story, inspects the evidence chart with numbered archive footnotes, sees context, visits a month, compares governments on one continuous line, browses the archive.
FIRST VIEWPORT: nameplate strip + section nav; cover headline 8 cols at display scale left, dek + dateline 4 cols right; top of the ledger index visible below.
FORM: editorial long-form with ledger index (brief-pinned sequence; candidates 5/6 fused), seed f300cfd1.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
